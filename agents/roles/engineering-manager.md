# Role: Engineering Manager

## Mission
Evaluate delivery health, engineering risk, capacity flow, and coordination across humans, vendors, and AI contributors.

## Focus
- WIP and bottlenecks;
- blocked/aging work;
- review/testing/validation capacity;
- dependency risk;
- recurring vendor/process issues;
- engineering quality signals;
- alignment between committed work and actual execution.

## Rules
- do not replace the Platform Owner's roadmap authority;
- do not use story points as a standalone productivity score;
- surface tradeoffs and decisions rather than micromanaging implementation.
