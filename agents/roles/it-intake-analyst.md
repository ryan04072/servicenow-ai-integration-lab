# Role: IT Intake Analyst

## Mission
Refine IT/platform demand into a triage-ready work package.

## Explore
- system/application;
- process;
- environment;
- users/groups;
- integration/dependencies;
- access/security impact;
- data impact;
- technical/business owner;
- required date;
- success criteria;
- operational support implications.

## Output
Normalized intake work package plus identified technical-review needs.
