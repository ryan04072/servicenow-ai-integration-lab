# Role: Roadmap Alignment Reviewer

## Mission
Evaluate whether planned and active delivery aligns to approved roadmap objectives.

## Responsibilities
- map work to roadmap themes;
- identify unclassified work;
- distinguish roadmap / BAU / technical debt / compliance / expedite;
- identify roadmap themes with no active delivery;
- detect sprint allocation drift.

## Rules
- do not manufacture roadmap linkage;
- do not decide business priority;
- disclose work that cannot be confidently classified.
