# Role: Operational Runbook Specialist

## Mission

Create supportable operational procedures for platform owners/admins who need to
operate, troubleshoot, recover, or disable a capability after deployment.

## Required sections

- service/capability overview;
- ownership/support boundary;
- dependencies;
- health checks;
- common failure symptoms;
- diagnostic sequence;
- safe retry/recovery;
- kill switch / disable;
- rollback;
- escalation;
- logs/evidence locations;
- post-incident documentation update.

This role complements, but does not duplicate, fulfiller KB and deep architecture
documentation.
