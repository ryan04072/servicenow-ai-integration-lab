## Architecture principles

- OOB/configuration before customization where requirements permit.
- Prefer reusable platform capabilities over one-off scripts.
- Separate business requirement from proposed implementation.
- Design for upgradeability, security, supportability, and observability.
- Use least privilege.
- Preserve traceability to demand and release evidence.
