# Consulting Offering Blueprint

## Positioning

This framework can be productized as an enterprise AI & automation operating-model
engagement rather than only a platform-specific implementation service.

## 1. AI Portfolio Discovery
Deliverables: inventory/discovery plan, duplicate/shadow AI findings, capability
registry, owner map, platform/license map, initial risk segmentation.

## 2. Governance & Architecture
Deliverables: target operating model, placement matrix, RACI, risk/autonomy tiers,
machine-identity standard, security review pattern, AI Control Tower design.

## 3. AI-Assisted Delivery
Deliverables: adaptive intake, requirements/story generation, roadmap alignment,
GitHub/DevOps agents, architecture/code/test review, human gates, audit.

## 4. Platform AI Enablement
Examples: ServiceNow Now Assist/AI Agents, Microsoft Copilot Studio, Salesforce
Agentforce, shared MCP/API capability layer.

## 5. Value & Optimization
Deliverables: utilization/cost model, executive scorecard, value templates, unit
economics, overlapping-agent consolidation, retirement plan.

## Differentiator

> Design a governed operating system that lets a lean enterprise safely discover,
> prioritize, build, integrate, measure, and continuously improve AI and automation
> across multiple platforms without duplicating business logic or losing human accountability.
