# Executive Brief + Intelligent Intake

## Workflow A — Executive Sprint Brief

```text
Trigger (manual/scheduled)
  ↓
Read ADO sprint + backlog exceptions
  ↓
Read approved roadmap
  ↓
Optional GitHub evidence
  ↓
Roadmap alignment
  ↓
Flow / validation analysis
  ↓
Executive brief
  ↓
Human review
  ↓
Discuss / send
```

### Default briefing

- overall health;
- roadmap progress;
- sprint status;
- material accomplishments;
- validation/WIP exceptions;
- blockers and aging;
- decisions needed;
- upcoming milestones.

### Important

A leadership brief is not a list of 40 validation items or 170 backlog items.
It should aggregate and surface exceptions.

## Workflow B — Intelligent Intake

```text
New request
 ↓
Clarify problem/outcome
 ↓
Search duplicate/existing work
 ↓
Map to roadmap / platform
 ↓
Draft story + acceptance criteria
 ↓
Recommend classification
 ↓
Identify dependencies
 ↓
Human triage
 ↓
Backlog
```

The AI may recommend priority inputs.
Humans own:
- final priority;
- sprint commitment;
- expedite decisions;
- funding;
- architecture acceptance.
