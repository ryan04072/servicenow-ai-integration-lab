# Governance Crosswalk — NIST AI RMF / ISO 42001

This is an implementation mapping, not a certification claim.

## NIST AI RMF

### GOVERN
Covered by policy mapping, RACI, inventory, lifecycle, risk-based autonomy,
identity/security standards, incident/accountability, and retirement.

### MAP
Covered by use-case intake, workload placement, context/data mapping,
capability registry, risk classification, and impact identification.

### MEASURE
Covered by evals, quality monitoring, adoption, utilization, cost/value,
outcome measurement, and runtime traces.

### MANAGE
Covered by risk treatment, human gates, kill switch, incident response,
remediation, fallback, and retirement.

## ISO/IEC 42001

The operating model is consistent with management-system concepts such as defined
policy/objectives, roles/responsibilities, risk/opportunity management, lifecycle
controls, monitoring/measurement, and continual improvement.

Formal conformity requires a dedicated gap assessment against the licensed standard.
