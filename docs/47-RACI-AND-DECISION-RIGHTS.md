# RACI and Decision Rights

## Purpose

Agents can recommend. Named human roles remain accountable.

Populate `config/governance/raci.template.yaml`.

## Decisions to assign

- roadmap ownership;
- business priority;
- sprint commitment;
- platform architecture;
- ServiceNow engineering standards;
- security approval;
- AI governance/policy;
- tool access;
- data access;
- vendor performance;
- merge approval;
- validation/UAT;
- change approval;
- production release;
- exception handling;
- autonomy promotion.

## Rule

A workflow may automate preparation and evidence collection without changing who
owns the decision.

## Example

```text
Architecture recommendation
AI: Responsible for analysis
Platform Owner/Architect: Accountable for decision
Security: Consulted where required
Stakeholders: Informed
```
