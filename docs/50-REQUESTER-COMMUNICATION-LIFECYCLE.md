# Requester Communication Lifecycle

## Goal

A strong internal operating model must still feel transparent to the requester.

## Communication states

### Received
Acknowledge request and provide tracking reference.

### Clarification needed
Ask targeted questions with context.

### Accepted for evaluation
Explain that acceptance into intake is not the same as sprint commitment.

### Decision
Communicate:
- approved;
- duplicate;
- not proceeding;
- future roadmap;
- more information needed.

### Planned
Communicate expected planning horizon when available.

### In progress
Provide meaningful milestone updates without exposing unnecessary engineering detail.

### Delivered
Explain what changed and what the requester should do.

### Outcome review
When appropriate, confirm whether the requested outcome was achieved.

## AI use

AI may draft communications.

Human review should remain for:
- sensitive decisions;
- prioritization disputes;
- denials;
- executive/high-impact requests;
- material scope changes.
