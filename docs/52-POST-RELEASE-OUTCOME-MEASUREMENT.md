# Post-Release Outcome Measurement

## Delivery is not the end state

A technically successful release can still fail to solve the original problem.

## Close the loop

At intake, capture success criteria.

After release, where appropriate, compare:

```text
Expected outcome
vs.
Observed outcome
```

## Potential evidence

- adoption;
- request volume;
- incident volume;
- manual steps removed;
- time saved;
- error rate;
- deflection;
- user feedback;
- platform performance;
- control/risk improvement.

## Outcome states

- achieved;
- partially achieved;
- not achieved;
- not yet measurable;
- measurement unavailable.

## Feed learning back

Outcome evidence may inform:
- roadmap;
- future architecture;
- process design;
- technical debt;
- precedent;
- AI eval cases.

Do not automatically rewrite standards based on one outcome.
