# Executive AI Scorecard

Keep the executive view small.

## Portfolio
- discovered AI assets;
- managed/unmanaged assets;
- assets without accountable owner;
- assets by risk tier;
- new/retired assets.

## Adoption
- active users;
- AI actions;
- top adopted capabilities;
- underused paid capabilities.

## Quality & Reliability
- task completion / acceptance;
- quality/safety score;
- failed tool/action rate;
- AI-related incidents/cases;
- rework/correction rate.

## Security & Governance
- high-risk open findings;
- overdue assessments;
- excessive-permission findings;
- kill-switch/security events;
- open remediation actions.

## Cost
- total AI cost;
- cost by platform/provider;
- cost by business unit/capability;
- trend.

## Value
- capacity hours released;
- hard-dollar savings/avoidance;
- net AI return;
- cycle-time improvements;
- top value-producing assets;
- assets with cost but no demonstrated value.

## Decisions required
Surface only material decisions: expand, consolidate, remediate, retire, or investigate.
