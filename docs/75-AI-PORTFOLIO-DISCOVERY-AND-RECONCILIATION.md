# AI Portfolio Discovery and Reconciliation

## Objective

Maintain one defensible inventory rather than relying only on self-reporting.

## Sources

- planned AI intake;
- ServiceNow-native AI inventory;
- AI Control Tower connectors;
- Service Graph discovery;
- cloud/provider APIs;
- platform inventories;
- security discovery;
- manual registration when automation is unavailable.

## Reconciliation process

For every discovered asset:
1. identify runtime/platform;
2. identify owner if known;
3. link to existing asset/capability if duplicate;
4. determine business purpose;
5. determine data/tool access;
6. determine risk;
7. decide managed/unmanaged disposition;
8. assign follow-up;
9. record retirement/remediation when needed.

## Inventory quality indicators

Track:
- assets with owner;
- assets with business purpose;
- assets with risk classification;
- assets with lifecycle state;
- assets with cost/value mapping;
- unmanaged assets;
- duplicate/overlapping agents;
- assets with no recent usage;
- assets with active permissions but no owner;
- assets pending retirement.

Discovery is a continuous control; cadence should reflect organizational risk and rate of change.
