# Executive IT Intelligence Blueprint

## Goal

Provide leadership with plain-language answers without requiring them to know
tables, reports, dashboards, or query syntax.

## Do not begin with the chatbot

Begin with the metric contract.

### Tier 1 — certified metrics
Small, trusted set of leadership KPIs.

### Tier 2 — diagnostic dimensions
Service, assignment group, category, priority, application, vendor, time.

### Tier 3 — operational evidence
Incidents, problems, changes, alerts, delivery work, major-incident records.

### Tier 4 — AI narrative
"What changed?", "why might it matter?", "what requires attention?"

## Suggested leadership domains

### Service health
- major incidents;
- availability/reliability;
- service-impacting alerts.

### Support health
- volume;
- aging;
- MTTR;
- SLA;
- reopen;
- assignment group load.

### Quality / problem management
- recurring incidents;
- problem candidates;
- repeat causes;
- knowledge gaps.

### Change health
- change success;
- failed/backed-out change;
- incidents correlated with recent changes.

### Delivery health
- roadmap allocation;
- sprint flow;
- validation backlog;
- blockers;
- release throughput.

## Response contract

An executive answer should contain:
1. headline;
2. metric/date range;
3. material change;
4. likely drivers only when evidence supports them;
5. decision/action if required;
6. link to source visualization/evidence.

## Example

> Incident backlog increased 14% over the last 30 days, concentrated in two
> assignment groups. The largest contributor is aging access requests. No
> material increase in P1/P2 volume is present. Review staffing/automation for
> those queues.

The exact numbers must come from certified analytics, not model calculation.
