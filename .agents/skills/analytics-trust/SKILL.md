# Skill: Trusted Conversational Analytics

Use when answering management/executive questions from ServiceNow analytics.

1. Identify the requested metric.
2. Resolve it against the certified KPI catalog.
3. If no certified definition exists, disclose ambiguity before answering.
4. Retrieve the metric/visualization from Platform Analytics/Performance Analytics.
5. Preserve date range, filters and breakdowns.
6. Let AI interpret the result; do not let AI invent the metric.
7. Link to source evidence.
